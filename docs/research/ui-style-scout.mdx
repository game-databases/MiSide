Brief [T2-ui-style-scout.md](../briefs/T2-ui-style-scout.md), executed
2026-08-24 (attempt 4 — prior attempts died reading full-res JPEGs, so this
pass never opens one in context: 900 px thumbnails were rendered with the

[`design/refs/screenshots/thumbs/`](../../design/refs/screenshots/) and only
those were viewed; every hex below was extracted programmatically from the
originals via `Image.quantize` / `getpixel` box-medians). Fulfils FRAMEWORK

from the game's own UI — under the dual mandate of
[design-standard.md](../../../../_foundation/design-standard.md) §1 and its
§5 feel bar.

Evidence: 22 images already on disk (9 store shots `steam-ss0..8`, 2 demo
shots `demo-ds0/1`, 3 trailer thumbs, 6 community captures `comm-*`, plus
prior attempts' derived crop composites). Provenance:
[`credits.jsonl`](../../design/refs/screenshots/credits.jsonl) (this pass
appended the rows that were missing — see [§8](#8-method--provenance)).

The one-line read: **MiSide's chrome is pill-shaped candy pink floating on
deep purple space, and its horror face is the same shapes lit by CRT green
and alarm red.** The site should be dark plum-first with a magenta-pink
accent system, rounded-everything geometry, and glitch treatments reserved
for "corrupted" data.

---

## 1. Dominant palettes

### 1.1 Cozy mode

Two cozy registers coexist and the site needs both:

**Candy register (the game's actual UI surface).** Full-bleed pink menus and
dialogue backdrops — `steam-ss2` quantized: `#e06eb0` 23%, `#df81b0` 21%,
`#e091b4` 20%, `#df5eae` 18%; direct samples `page-bg #df72b3`, header pill
`#fda3c7`, category pill `#f8b3d2`, scrollbar track `#de7bb1` [all sampled,
steam-ss2]. The flat dialogue backdrop in `demo-ds1` is a single hot
magenta-pink: `#cc2a73` at 65% coverage [sampled]. Panel fills sit lighter:
`#ffc3dd`, `#f59dc0`, `#de7da3` [sampled, steam-ss2 panel region]; the
name tag is pure hot pink `#ff009c` [sampled, demo-ds1].

**Cream register (the apartment itself).** Warm wood + cream interiors —
`steam-ss1` quantized: `#cb9885` 21%, `#e7bbab` 15%, `#fce1d5` 13%
[sampled]. This is the "daylight" face; it is scenery, not chrome — the
game never puts UI on cream.

### 1.2 The purple constant

Behind every cozy screen sits deep purple space: `steam-ss1` HUD backdrop
`#5a1565` direct, quantized `#4a1d5a` 24% / `#601f59` 11% [sampled]. The
translucent dialogue band over a dark room reads `#54175d` / `#4f1262`
[sampled, steam-ss1] — i.e. the same pink band at low alpha over purple.
Purple is the connective tissue between the two faces: even the fog horror
scene `steam-ss7` is mauve, not grey — quantized `#704660` 26%, `#82618b`
15%, direct `fog #785a8c` [sampled]. The dusk scene `steam-ss6` cools toward
blue-lavender: `#586174` 29%, `#97a2b4` 27%, `#272d38` 18% [sampled].

### 1.3 Horror / glitch mode

- **Machine-room black + alarm red + CRT green** — `steam-ss8` quantized:
  `#1b0116` 31%, `#03000a` 23%, `#300a1a` 17% [sampled]; direct: red light
  strip `#fc0f43`, doorway red `#813e47`, screen dark `#140316` [sampled].
- **Crimson chapel** — `comm-dialogue-horror-ptbr`: lattice windows
  `#ff2b58`, frame `#fc3258` [sampled].
- **Liminal checkerboard void** — `comm-checkerboard-void`: grey-lavender
  tiles `#6e748a` 33% / `#646581` / `#676b88`, one teal object `#407982`
  [sampled] — desaturated purple again, never neutral grey.
- **VHS-washed pastel** — `demo-ds0` (glitch mirror scene) quantized:
  `#b2abb0` 27%, `#d5c1c2` 16%, `#998393` 16%, `#a7bfcf` 15%, direct
  `pastel-wall #c9c8cd` [sampled] — the horror of the cozy palette drained
  and fringed, not a new palette.
- **LCD minigame** — `comm-minigame-lcd-defeat`: screen `#101010`/`#110f14`
  with olive cast `#22492b`, pixel text green `#08cb05` [sampled].

## 2. Typography impressions

- **UI sans: rounded, friendly, bold.** Dialogue lines ("Thanks a lot!",
  "Nice to meet you, rrr!" — `steam-ss1`, `demo-ds1`), name tags, and button
  labels are a rounded-terminal sans at medium/bold weight; no thin weights,
  no serifs anywhere in the corpus. Mixed case for prose, **UPPERCASE for
  section headers** ("MINI GAMES", "ELECTRONICS", "PROCESSING" — `steam-ss2`)
  and key labels ("SPACE" — `demo-ds1`).
- **White-on-pink with soft lift.** Chrome text is white `#fffcff`
  [sampled, demo-ds1] on pink pills, with a subtle darker-pink/drop lift
  (visible on the SPACE pill and header pills in `steam-ss2`).
- **Pixel font as flavor.** The handheld console renders `SCORE: 26` /
  `DEFEAT` in a chunky pixel font, green `#08cb05` on near-black
  [sampled, comm-minigame-lcd-defeat]. This is the game's "machine voice"
  and the natural font for score/telemetry-flavored data on the site.
- **Keycap letters.** `W/A/S/D`/`ESC` hints are single bold white letters on
  small magenta rounded squares (`comm-minigame-tv-penguins`,
  `comm-minigame-lcd-defeat`).
- **Subtitles.** `comm-dialogue-horror-ptbr` shows purple rounded-sans
  subtitles with a heavy dark outline; `comm-horror-caption-band` shows the
  caption style as white rounded text on a maroon gradient pill (`#84504f` →
  `#934053` [sampled]). Whether the purple subtitle fill is game-native or a
  community overlay is [unverified] — the pill-caption style in
  `comm-horror-caption-band` and `demo-ds0` ("I'm washing") is consistent
  enough to treat as native.

## 3. UI chrome patterns

- **Dialogue box** (`steam-ss1`, `demo-ds1`, `comm-dialogue-sorry-sweetheart`):
  full-width bottom band, translucent pink fill (reads `#ec6796` over the
  magenta backdrop, `#54175d` over a dark room — alpha, not a solid),
  fully-rounded corners; **name tag pill** overlapping the band's top-left
  (`Mita`/`Мита` — locale-stable chrome); **advance prompt** as a pink
  "SPACE" pill at the band's top-right.
- **Choices/captions** (`demo-ds0`): mid-screen choice pill "TAKE PILLS"
  in vivid magenta-purple `#c02dbb`/`#872cb2` [sampled] with keycap-style
  brackets; bottom caption band translucent dark-pink `#866087` [sampled].
- **HUD** (`steam-ss1`, crop composite `.crops-ui.jpg`): top-left circular
  portrait in a ring with a **level chip** `#ff5598` [sampled] and two
  **pill gauges** (white outline, pale-pink fill `#ffdfe5` → `#f9bacb`
  [sampled], icon in a circle at the left cap); top-right **currency pill**
  (coin + count) and a circular avatar button.
- **Menu/list** (`steam-ss2`): full-bleed patterned pink background (a
  repeating wallpaper of hearts and household icons — also behind
  `comm-dialogue-sorry-sweetheart`); category **panels** (lighter pink,
  rounded ~24 px) each with an uppercase **header pill** and a row of three
  **square cells** (rounded ~12–16 px, dark cartridge-style thumbnails,
  `#bb70a8` [sampled] shadowed) with per-cell counts; rounded scrollbar
  track with a lock glyph at its end.
- **Key hints**: keycap chip + gradient label pill pairs
  (`comm-minigame-tv-penguins`: `W UP / A LEFT / S DOWN / D RIGHT`;
  `comm-minigame-lcd-defeat`: `ESC` + "FINISH PLAYING" pill, gradient
  `#b338b1 → #d682b1` [sampled]).
- **LCD console** (`comm-minigame-lcd-defeat`): blue plastic shell, dark
  olive screen, green pixel text, yellow hex buttons — a physical-object
  framing of a data surface.

Geometry summary: **everything is a pill or a rounded square** — no sharp
corners in any UI crop; radius family ≈ full (tags/pills/keycaps), ~24 px
(panels, dialogue band), ~12–16 px (cells, chips), all at 1080p scale.

## 4. Glitch / error motifs

1. **VHS banding + chromatic aberration** — `demo-ds0` (and crop composites
   `.crops-ui2/3.jpg`): horizontal displacement slices, magenta/green
   fringing on edges, scanline noise over the whole frame. The single most
   reusable motif: a "corruption hover" for compromised entities.
2. **Texture explosion** — full-frame dissolve into RGB confetti
   (`.ugc2/u16.jpg`, crop in `.crops-ui2.jpg`). Reads as "the world is
   failing"; good as a 404/error-state texture, too loud for routine accents.
3. **Red corruption streaks / glyph columns** — `steam-ss8` (vertical red
   glyph strip, red light bars `#fc0f43`), `.ugc2/u11.jpg` (red streak
   corridor). Danger/glitch accent language.
4. **CRT green terminals** — `steam-ss8` wall screens, LCD minigame text
   `#08cb05` [sampled]. The "machine truth" voice — right for raw-data
   surfaces (build IDs, pipeline output).
5. **Liminal void rooms** — `comm-checkerboard-void` checkerboard, `.ugc2/u27.jpg`
   purple void: emptiness rendered as a *designed* space (matches
   design-standard §5.1 "honesty without apology" — empty wells can be void-
   patterned rather than grey).

## 5. Light vs dark default

**Dark-first: a deep purple-plum, not black and not grey.** Reasons, all
sampled: the game's own chrome lives on purple space `#4a1d5a`/`#5a1565`
(`steam-ss1`); its horror register bottoms out at near-black purple
`#140316`/`#03000a` (`steam-ss8`); pink/magenta accents (`#ff009c`,
`#cc2a73`, `#fda3c7`) are mid-to-high-chroma and need a dark field to carry;
FRAMEWORK §2.11 mandates dark-first. Pure black would kill the purple
constant that makes MiSide *not* a generic dark wiki; grey-neutral would
break the "even the fog is mauve" finding from `steam-ss7`. A warm-cream
secondary surface (`#fce1d5`/`#e7bbab` — the apartment register, `steam-ss1`)
is worth keeping as an optional light theme for long reading pages, never
the default.

## 7. Anti-genericism check

What makes a MiSide page instantly *not* a generic dark wiki (proposals only;
build pipeline decides):

1. **Purple, not dark-grey.** The base field is `#140316 → #4a1d5a` purple.
   One glance separates it from every grey-wiki; it is the game's own
   "space behind the apartment" (§1.2).
2. **Pill-shaped chrome everywhere.** Name-tag pills, keycap chips, gradient
   pills, fully-rounded dialogue bands (§3) — sharp-cornered cards would
   read as someone else's site.
3. **Mita-keyed colour identity.** Each Mita variant carries a palette
   (blue-purple-haired Mita red/blue in `steam-ss4/5`; white-haired Mita on
   black `.ugc2/u12.jpg`; sketch-style glasses Mita `.ugc2/u25.jpg`; red-lit
   chapel Mita `comm-dialogue-horror-ptbr`). Per design-standard §5.1
   "colour is identity": entity pages re-key accent + art glow to their
   Mita's palette, not a shared grey card.
4. **Cartridge cards.** Category panels with header pill + square
   cartridge-style cells with counts (`steam-ss2`) map 1:1 to any
   grid-of-entities page (items, minigames, endings).
5. **Corruption as state, not decoration.** VHS banding + RGB split on hover
   for glitched/corrupted entities (§4.1); red `#fc0f43` for danger rows;
   CRT green `#08cb05` reserved for machine-voice data (build IDs, dump
   output); checkerboard-void pattern for honest empty wells (§4.5).
6. **Heart-wallpaper texture.** The repeating hearts-and-objects wallpaper
   (`steam-ss1/2`, `comm-dialogue-sorry-sweetheart`) as a low-contrast
   section background — cozy register in one move.
7. **Keycap `kbd` styling.** `W/A/S/D`/`ESC`/`SPACE` chips (§3) as the
   site's keyboard-hint language — game-native and instantly recognizable.
8. **The tension itself.** Every page should hold both faces — cozy pill
   chrome on purple space, one glitch treatment never more than a hover
   away. All-candy reads as a fan shrine; all-horror reads as an edgy wiki;
   the game is the cut between them.

## 8. Method & provenance

- Viewed: 22 thumbnails in `design/refs/screenshots/thumbs/` (900 px wide,
  Pillow LANCZOS, q78) — full-res files never entered context (attempts 1–3
  died there).
- Sampled: `Image.quantize(6, MEDIANCUT)` for dominance; 9×9-px box medians
  and region quantizes for chrome points, coordinates mapped from thumbs
  (×2.1333 for 1920-wide, ×2.8444 for 2560-wide). Two early probes hit
  portrait art instead of chrome (ss1 name-tag/SPACE regions are
  skin-contaminated in §sampling logs); those points were re-taken from
  `demo-ds1` and region-dominance scans, so every hex cited above traces to
  a printed sample.
- `credits.jsonl` gaps closed this pass: `.ugc2/u01..u30.jpg` +
  `.ugcpool/ugc01..30.jpg` credited against the 30 URLs in the prior
  attempt's `.ugc_urls.txt` — the positional mapping is content-verified for
  six anchors by MD5 (u01/u06/u24/u27/u29/u30 are byte-identical to the
  promoted `comm-*` files); the remaining 24 pool rows are positional
  [unverified per-file]. Derived artifacts (`.contact-*.jpg`,
  `.crops-ui*.jpg`, `.probe-*.png`, `thumbs/*`) got `kind:"derived"` rows
  naming their sources.
- Not done (no retry loops): no in-game font files inspected — typography
  stays impression-level (§2); trailer thumbs (`steam-teaser`,
  `steam-movietrailer*`) glanced via `.contact-official.jpg` only, too small
  to sample.
