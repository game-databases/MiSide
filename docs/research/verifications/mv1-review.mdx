### F-1 · MEDIUM — AC MV-0.4's ledger arithmetic is unsatisfiable at kind granularity

Spec §3.2(c): *"The rerun's `pending_families` must enumerate every
`marker_eligible:true` kind with its deferral reason"*; AC MV-0.4:
*"eligible-row arithmetic (709) reconciles against families + emitted
rows."* Kind-level families cannot partition the 709:

- `travel_gate` (46 rows, measured) splits into 19 `Scene_Load`
  (save_point-owned) + 14 `Trigger_Teleport` + 13 `Player_Teleport`
  (no owner) — one kind, two destinies;
- `cartridge` (25) splits 21 emittable + 4 OQ-8 pets;
- `profile_document` contributes 11 emitted rows that are **not** poi
  rows at all, so they sit outside the 709 yet must appear in "families +
  emitted rows".

As written, the reconciliation identity is undefined (and under the
natural reading, false). Fix is one paragraph: state that families may
key finer than kind (class axis allowed, e.g. `save_point` ↔
`Scene_Load`), and pin the arithmetic as Σ(family poi_rows) = 709 with
emitted-marker counts carried per family (profile family sourced from
DS-5, outside the poi pool). Without this the CodeWriter must invent the
identity to pass MV-0.4 — exactly the R-D-C1 failure mode.

### F-2 · MEDIUM — OQ-7 (`minigame_access`) has no marker shape it can legally use

OQ-7's premise verifies (17/17 minigame keys resolve; 76 edges), but the
carrier edge binds `(minigame, container)` by class co-presence —
`to:"scene-class-family@level9"` — while the 70 `minigame_access` poi
rows are `MinigamesController` instances, ~3 per level across 24 levels,
and **level1's 3 rows are covered by no carrier edge** (edges span
level2–level23 only; measured). Two consequences:

1. A per-instance `poi_id` anchor (mandatory in the §4.1 shape for every
   non-profile row) is not derivable — multiple controller rows per level
   and the edge's carrier classes are not controller rows. Emission needs
   the scene-granular escape hatch that §3.2(b) defines *only* for DS-5
   placements (`placement_source`-keyed rows), or an `instance_census`
   grouping — declare it before implementation.
2. The container must be split out of `scene-class-family@levelN`
   emitter-side; string-splitting a compound enum downstream is a
   derivation the frontend-consumes law forbids.

Also add level1's orphan rows to the ledger reasons so F-1's arithmetic
stays total.

### F-5 · LOW — the §2 dependency diagram contradicts the table it illustrates

The ASCII graph draws arrowheads **into M2 from the M4 lane**
(`▲╚── M4`), reading as M4→M2, while the table (and the caption "M4
consumes focus URLs + marker rows") say M4 depends on M0+M2. Table is
acyclic and complete (checked: every module's inputs are emitted by a

### F-6 · LOW — registry v2 should be declared additive

§3.2(d) lists what v2 *adds* (`display_label_loc`, per-kind counts,
`role`, `status`) but never says existing `zoom:[1,4]`,
`coordinate-transform`, `imagery` survive — and §5's clamped pan/zoom
reads `registry.zoom` (verified present today). One clause: "v2 extends
the v1 registry object; no v1 field is removed."

### F-7 · MEDIUM — M4 modules render inferred joins with no mechanism disclosure

The map popover shows `placement.mechanism` ("hard | logic | inferred",
§4.1) — but the entity-page module table (§7) sources `mita` scenes from
`character--scene-membership.jsonl`, whose 12 rows are measured
`mechanism:"inferred", status:"modeled"`, and prescribes no provenance
display. Rendering an inferred co-presence as flat "found in" fact is
inconsistent with the spec's own popover law and the pack's
honesty-vocabulary discipline (data-contracts §1; the mechanism column
exists in the relink precisely to be surfaced). Cheapest fix: one line in
§7 — modules carry `mechanism`/`status` through when ≠ hard, same as the
popover.

### F-8 · LOW — no RTL leg on the new controls

Chrome ships `ar`, `ar-EG`, `fa` (verified in
`site/src/i18n/chrome/`). MV-5 tests label parity, MV-7 tests 375 px —
neither exercises the switcher/popover/side-sheet under RTL mirroring,
and Leaflet pan-zoom direction is a known RTL trap. One scripted-trace
assertion (or an explicit "RTL deferred, tracked" note) closes it.
