### HIGH-2 — CL-1 predicate unsatisfiable post-trigger: ACCEPT (prior-pin record design, option b)

Measured confirmation: fingerprints.json holds exactly one generation —
distinct build_ids `{19029065}` over all 84 rows, parsed this pass; the
§0.1-quoted patch_day law is replacement ("a new buildId invalidates every
fingerprint; rerun re-pins them"), not accumulation. Therefore on trigger
day the committed corpus legitimately holds ONE distinct build_id PLUS
valid changesets ⇒ "<2 distinct build_ids" fails every correct post-trigger
build, and the only state satisfying the gate is the accidental mid-rerun
mixed-id transient — noise keyed to commit timing. The gate red-flags its
own success state. Confirmed exactly as reviewed.

**Design decision: option (b), the written prior-pin record — with option
(a)'s structural half retained unchanged.** Grounding in the fingerprint
discipline:

1. **Replacement semantics are the law the spec rides.** The gate keyed on
   fingerprints.json id-count contradicts the very §0.1 quote the spec
   calls load-bearing. The pressure escape — keeping stale rows in
   fingerprints.json to hold ≥2 ids — would corrupt CL-2's recomputed-sha
   == fingerprints.json equality and pollute stamp-class accounting. A
   separate lane is the only clean home for pre-patch truth.
2. **The pack's proof style is disk-verifiable offline.** Every CL proof
   method in §11 is mechanical/sandbox-replayable. Option (a)'s "produced
   by M2 in the same run" is ephemeral process state — unverifiable after
   the run ends, so its proof decays; it is the one AC whose acceptance
   could never be re-proven from the committed corpus.
3. **Tree N−1 is deleted after diffing completes** (data-acquisition
   keep-until-diffed law, ridden at §4.2 step 2). Without a written
   witness, the from-side of every changeset rests on nothing durable —
   unfalsifiable provenance, precisely what the anti-fake architecture
   exists to forbid. The pin record IS that witness, hashed from tree N−1
   directly under fingerprints conventions, immune to re-pin ordering.
4. Option (a)'s structural authorship law survives as-is regardless: M1
   still structurally cannot emit `evidence_class:"computed"` (§3.3) —
   that half was never the broken part.

Mechanics pinned in the fix order (F4/F5): schema
`miside.changelog.prior_pin/1` at
`extracted/data/changelog/_pins/<from>-to-<to>.json`, written by M2 at
trigger time BEFORE tree N−1 is released, gate re-keyed to record-per-
changeset coherence, proofs re-cut both arms (refusal pre-trigger,
acceptance post-trigger) reusing CL-10's two-tree fixture.
