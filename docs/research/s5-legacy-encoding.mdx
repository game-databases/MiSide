## 1. The seven files

Same relative path in every case: `Data\Languages\<locale>\LocationDialogue Location12.txt`. All are small (998–1,408 B), LF-only, no trailing newline (category norm).

| English | 1,049 | 1 | 906 | `0a537461792068657265…` — `\nStay here.\nI think I dr…`, pure ASCII until stray byte |
| Filipino | 1,154 | 1 | 950 | `0a4469746f206b61206c616e67…` — `\nDito ka lang.\nMay naiwa…` |

| Serbian (Latin) | 1,017 | 13 | 41 | `…zaboravila ne` + `9a` + `to.` — legacy bytes start at line 1 |
| Slovak | 1,011 | 62 | 6 | `0a5a6f737461` `f2` `20747 52e…` → `\nZostaň tu.\nMyslím, že s…` under cp1250 |

**Two distinct failure classes, not one:** Croatian/English/Filipino/Indonesia/Vietnamese are valid UTF-8 except for **exactly one stray byte each — always `0xCC`, always in line 58** (the coordinates line, see §3). Serbian (Latin) and Slovak carry genuine runs of unconverted single-byte text (13 and 62 invalid segments).

## 2. Codec verdicts

Method: maximal-contiguous-segment isolation under strict UTF-8, then per-segment trial decode over 13 candidate codecs with **byte round-trip proof** (`decoded.encode(codec) == original_bytes`), then language-consistency check against the file's own UTF-8 context.

- **Slovak — cp1250 (Windows-1250), proven.** Every one of the 62 segments round-trips under cp1250 (iso-8859-2 agrees on all bytes present; mac-latin2/cp1251/koi8-r produce nonsense). Recovery yields correct Slovak orthography: `Zostaň tu.` · `Myslím, že som niečo stratila.` · `Poďme sa zahrať!` · `Si plyšový medvedík` · `Ukážem ti ako.` Independent confirmation: Slovak's own `0xCC` recovers to `Ě` under cp1250 — exactly the glyph Czech and Hungarian ship (already UTF-8-transcoded) at the same position of the same line.
- **Serbian (Latin) — cp1250 family, proven for every byte present.** All 13 segments are `9a`/`9e`/`8e` → š/ž/Ž (identical mapping in cp1250, cp1252, cp1254; cp1250 declared for vendor consistency with Slovak). Recovery reads as correct Serbian Latin: `zaboravila nešto` · `Želim svog medu!` · `Želi da se igra`.
- **The five single-`CC` files — NOT recoverable, by corpus evidence.** No codec gives `8⟦CC⟧54*` meaning (cp1250 `Ě`, cp1251 `М`, cp1252 `Ì`, koi8-r `л` — all nonsense inside coordinates). Line 58 is corrupted *fleet-wide*: the same source glyph ships as `Ì`×7, `М`×10 (RU/FR/DE/JA/ZH/KO/KK…), `Ě`×2, `M`×2, `I`×3, `Ъ`×1 (Pre-revolutionaryRussian: `8МЪ54*`), literal U+FFFD×3 (BG/IT/ES-Spain), raw `CC`×6. Each locale's translator tool transcoded its own ANSI codepage byte `0xCC`; the upstream glyph was destroyed before shipping. Decoding it would *fabricate* a character the corpus cannot justify.
- Erratum vs I-3 wording: cp1250 `9a`=š is correct; **`f2`=ň, not ô** (`ô` is `f4` — visible in `pôjdeme`).

Structural normality: **all 34 locales' `LocationDialogue Location12` split into exactly 71 lines** (`Counter({71: 34})`) — blank-line skeleton and `// Quest N (…)` comment lines (indices 25/31/38/44/49/60) align everywhere. The 7 files are structurally intact; only byte encoding differs. Post-recovery, Slovak/Serbian keep 71 lines, zero control characters.

## 3. Survey of all 34 dirs (would S5 trip again?)

Full byte-scan of 2,210 txt files: **exactly 7 non-UTF-8 files — the ones above.** No other category or locale carries the signature. Adjacent traps found while scanning:

- **36 files are UTF-8-BOM'd** (mostly Hungarian): `parse_category` keeps the BOM, so `line_index 0` text starts with `\ufeff` (verified in emitted `localization/Hungarian/CoreSoft.jsonl`). .NET's `StreamReader` strips BOMs, so in-game parity of first lines likely differs; join keys unaffected. Worth an F-note when touched next.
- **Line 58 is defective in all 34 locales** (census above) — even healthy-parsed locales carry mojibake verbatim. Nothing for S5 to do under the verbatim rule; the future dialogue dataset wants a known-defects note.
- CRLF appears in 1,802 files — `splitlines()` handles both; no impact.

**Ledger pollution (new, fixes itself under option b):** because English's own Location12 failed to parse, the *reference* category set lost the category — 27 locales now show phantom `extra_vs_reference: ["LocationDialogue Location12"]` rows in `_ledger/locale-delta.jsonl`, corrupting R-E1-3's skew semantics for every locale.
