This page compiles the secret surfaces MiSide actually routes today: the five puzzle-indexed paper fragments of Level 13, the novella surface, and the fourteen character profile photos. Everything below is read from the extracted client of build 19029065 (v0.93L); where the client proves nothing, this page says so instead of inventing copy.

Which secrets are actually routed?

Three families of hidden papers are reachable through routed pages right now:

  • World papers — six rows: the five paper_part fragments plus one novella_surface. They live under /lore, re-spaced ids and all, because the client ships no display titles for them.
  • Profile documents — fourteen rows, one photo page per character, all bound to the single mita-profiles collectible set.
  • Books — eight shelf books with their own gallery; they are dressing-layer catalogue rows rather than secrets, so this page only points at them.

The 160 note rows are deliberately absent from the linked lists below. No routed kind reaches them yet — widening that vocabulary is a governed contract change, not an editorial decision — so until then notes appear here only as plain text mentions, never as links.

Are the books and notes secrets too?

Books are shelf dressing with a gallery: eight volumes whose art exists in all 34 client locales except the two Chinese variants, which ship four covers each (the Location19 pages are absent there). They are catalogued, not hunted.

The 160 notes stay catalogued-only: their placement containers and event wiring are measured, their serialized text mechanism is unresolved on every row, and no routed kind reaches them yet.
