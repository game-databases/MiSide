Every routed page on this database resolves through contracts extracted from
the Steam client build 19029065 (v0.93L). With the guides and news sections
opening, here is what that corpus pins today, in numbers taken straight from
the dataset headers.

## Scenes and points of interest

The scene registry holds 24 containers, from the boot and title screens to the
23 story-carrying levels. Beneath them sits a catalogue of 986 point-of-interest
rows: 298 carry inline positions from the level census, 76 were resolved by
driving the running client, and 612 hold no position yet — those render as
explicitly unplaced rather than guessed coordinates. Level 13 alone ("She Just
Wants to Sleep") carries the five puzzle-indexed paper fragments this site's
first guide walks through.

## Dialogue and endings

The dialogue layer decompresses to 2,839+ nodes across 19 carrier levels; the
ending graph contributes 371 choice nodes and 1,555 branch edges feeding four
routed endings. Safe-window percentages stay community-labeled until the
derivation proves them — the fences travel with the pages.

## Collectibles

Cartridges: 23 rows, each bound to its pickup container and, where the client
pins it, the character it depicts — Short-haired Mita's cartridge among them.
Minigames: 17 playable rows plus their carrier edges into specific scenes.
Achievements: all 26 registry entries with global unlock percentages and their
ending joins — the "Safe of life" achievement rides the safe-opening route.

## Documents

Fourteen profile documents cover every photographed character; the world-paper
registry adds 166 rows — 160 notes whose serialized text remains unresolved on
every row, five paper fragments, one novella surface — plus eight shelf books
whose covers exist in 34 locales, four each in the two Chinese variants.

## Locales

All 34 client locales ship availability ledgers per category, so every entity
page knows exactly which of its strings exist where — pages omit rather than
mix languages, and the same ledger now also gates which articles appear in
which locale.
