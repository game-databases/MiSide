## Locale model (binding)

Identical pointer discipline to
[`dataset-characters.mdx`](dataset-characters.mdx): text resolves at render
time through `{category, line_index}` into
`extracted/localization/<locale>/<category>.jsonl`
(`GlobalLanguage.GetString(category, index)`); no locale copies ride in rows.

1. **TelevisionGames −1 offset pin (binding).** TV entry names resolve as
   `GetString("TelevisionGames", indexStringNameGame − 1)`: Fight → line 0
   `"Dairy Scandal"`, Pinguin → line 1 `"Penguin Piles"`. The identity offset
   is rejected mechanically: it indexes Pinguin out of range in all 34 locale
   dirs (category size = 2 everywhere) and lands Fight on the other game's
   slot where it does resolve. Both computations recompute per locale (AC-4).
2. Only the two TV rows carry `name_loc`. Prefab/carrier/tamagotchi rows have
   **no client display-name table**: `name_loc: null` is honest-missing — page
   titles compose from `client_key` + cited `community_alias`, never invented.
3. Availability derives from the four categories' presence per locale dir
   (34 dirs), reconciled against
   `localization/_ledger/locale-delta.jsonl`; the sole content skew is
   `MiniGame MakeManeken` = 34 lines in Arabic vs 35 elsewhere — render the
   declared explicit-filler state there. Deleting a category file in a scratch
   copy must flip that computed cell (AC-9).

**`cartridge_id` rule (pinned):** save_key lowercased with a hyphen inserted
at every letter↔digit boundary (`mtacap → mta-cap`, `plr1099 → plr-1099`,
`plr3vi → plr-3-vi`). Additive only — never replaces `save_key`. (The spec's
illustrative `plr-3` shorthand would collide keys; the pinned rule matches its
other example `mta-cap` verbatim and stays injective.)

## Relink files — edge-row schemas

First line `_meta` per file (family, pair, mechanism rules, pins,
relocation note). Every forward named edge has its mirrored inverse (doctrine
Principle one).

- **J1 `cartridge--character`**: `direction` · `edge_type`
  `depicts`\|`contains` · `from`/`to` over typed anchors
  `cartridge:<cartridge_id>` ↔ DS-1 ids · `c13_anchor`
  (`flashes:<save_key>`) reused **verbatim** from C13 · `member_character_id`
  · `mechanism: hard` · `status: modeled`.
- **J2 `cartridge--scene-placement`**: meta-only today (parked until the
  scenes dataset owns transforms/chapters). Its `_meta.placement_census`
  names this dataset's `pickup_ref` multiset as the single placement
  authority; DS-5 consumes the **11-row mita-side overlap** by reference —
  the 10 player-side pickups belong to this dataset alone (ds456-arbiter
  agenda-1 fence).
- **J3 `minigame--achievement`**: `from` `minigame:<id>`\|`null` · `to`
  `achievement:<ACHI_*>` · `mechanism` `hard` (dumped `AchievementGet` award
  site in `award_site{level,file,method,args_string,target_path_id,build_id}`)
  or `logic` (type-tag-only) · `attribution_evidence[]` states the mechanical
  tie when a target is named · `status` `modeled`\|`partial` with
  `missing_fields` naming the unblock for the six unresolved binds (tetro,
  racingfirst, logA, logB, hellwin, hellmegawin — zero dumped sites corpus-wide).
- **J4 `minigame--scene-carrier`**: edges over `(class family × container)`
  with `instance_census{bare,suffixed}` from the mb-dump filename census;
  `_meta.boilerplate_dedupe.minigamescontroller_partition` records the
  measured 48-dump → 19/16/12/1 hash partition (level2 group = 12 members).
- **J5 `minigame--outfit-unlock`**: pinguin→`outfit:Chirfns` **hard**
  (dual-grant site `level6/Dialogue_3DText_#7008.txt` also carries
  `AchievementGet("ACHI_PinguinTusim")`); pumpkin-clicker→`outfit:HellVamp`
  **logic/partial** (wiki-asserted, zero dumped `ClothCompleted` sites outside
  levels 5/6).
- **J6 `minigame--choice-condition`**: meta-only — measured absence (no
  console-game choice condition exists in `endings/choice_nodes.jsonl`);
  key space declared for DS-2 Part B consumers.

chapter attribution (scenes dataset); no scoring thresholds without dumped
evidence (R1 fence); no mutation of DS-1/DS-2 files by this stage; no
per-locale text copies beside the pointers.
