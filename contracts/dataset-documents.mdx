## Consumer rules

1. Never render note prose — there is none in the client corpus; notes render
   as placed interactables with their wiring.
2. Resolve all text through the locale pointers at render time; `name_en` is
   search glue only.
3. `chapter` renders as unknown until the scenes dataset lands; do not infer
   from containers.
4. Completion trackers key "Hi, Mita" off `achievement_sets` ∪
   `flash_save_key IS NULL AND placement_mechanism='story_granted'` (row 13
   counts; the predicate spans 12 keyed + 1 keyless row).
